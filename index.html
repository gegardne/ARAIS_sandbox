sculpt the site → place the net → pick a pattern → cut sheet (SVG, mm)

1Sculpt the site

Drag to build up or dig. Carve river and Raise ridge draw a narrow channel or crest along your stroke (width follows brush size). Shapes stamp a hill where you tap. The white outline is the unfolded lamp: each face reads the terrain underneath it.

2See it lit

A small emitter gives crisp shadows; slide it up to see what a frosted bulb does to the pattern. Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom.

Shape and pattern

Flowlines run downhill from each contour. Slopes facing the light get wide slots; slopes in shadow get thin ones or none.
